    Saved experiments

    Experiments snapshot labels and predictions. Changing the sliders recalculates those predictions locally in this app.

    Threshold explorer

    Lower cosine distance and higher gap require stronger full-face matches. Higher eye correlation filters eye-only suggestions. Eye search scans candidates at correlation ≥ 0.70 and always requires teacher review.

    How these measurements work

    Each prediction is paired with the label box containing its center. Boxes must not overlap. Correct means the expected identity was proposed; wrong means a different identity was proposed. Missed means no identity passed the selected rules for an enrolled student. Unknown rejection means no identity was proposed in an unfamiliar person’s box. Extra predictions are outside labels or duplicate predictions in one box.

    Recall is correct / enrolled labels. Precision is correct / all identity proposals, including wrong identities, unfamiliar-person false accepts and extra predictions. Unavailable method/photo pairs are excluded and counted explicitly. Duplicate full-face identity claims require review and do not pass the explorer. Eye-only metrics score review suggestions. These experiments use the whole active gallery and do not change the app’s matching settings.

    HOW A FACE BECOMES A RECORD

    Detect. Compare. Review. Save.

    Pretrained recognition models support the attendance workflow.

    1. 01

      Find the faces

      The selected detector locates faces, draws bounding boxes and aligns each face for comparison. RetinaFace is the default.

    2. 02

      Compare the details

      The selected embedding model turns a face into a 512-value embedding. FaceNet512 is the default. Cosine distance compares it with registered student templates.

    3. 03

      Review the evidence

      Matching rules filter candidates. Teachers review uncertain faces and confirm video proposals before they count toward attendance.

    4. 04

      Finalize the lecture

      The teacher checks the roster, makes corrections and locks the final attendance record for export.

    DESIGNED TO SUPPORT THE TEACHER

    Local processing. Thoughtful review.

    Face matching runs on this laptop, with reference photos and face templates stored in the project. Matching thresholds are provisional, and real classroom performance needs independent testing.

    If a face is covered, unclear or missed, teachers can confirm attendance through roll call. The experimental visible-eye search produces suggestions for review.
